FAQ

Common questions

Do you work on-site or remotely?

Support is delivered remotely over Teams, email and WhatsApp during UAE business hours. On-site visits in the UAE can be arranged when needed.

Which Dynamics 365 apps do you support?

Dynamics 365 Sales, Customer Service, Field Service and custom model-driven apps on Power Apps and Dataverse, including plugins, JavaScript, Power Automate flows and integrations.

What happens to hours I don’t use?

Unused hours roll over to the next month once. They don’t accumulate beyond that.

What is not included?

New modules, large integrations, data migrations and other project work. We quote these separately, at a fixed price where possible.

Is there a minimum contract?

Packages run for a minimum of 6 months, billed monthly or quarterly in advance.

Who did the original implementation — does it matter?

No. We support systems implemented by any company. We start with a short review of your environment so we understand how it was built.

Do you need admin access?

We need a user account with the right security role in your environment. Access stays under your control, and you can remove it at any time.
