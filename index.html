What we build

Four kinds of work, one way of working.

Most clients start with one and grow into the next when the first one earns its keep.

Billing & counter apps

Point of sale that a new hire can use on day one. Weight-based pricing, GST, thermal receipts, WhatsApp bills, day-end and month-end registers, stock that actually matches the shelf.

Suited to Bakeries, pharmacies, kirana and hardware stores, restaurants, service counters

Mobile apps

Android and iOS for the people who are never at a desk — delivery riders, field technicians, sales staff, warehouse teams. Works offline, syncs when the signal returns.

Suited to Field service, logistics, ride and rental platforms, retail chains

Websites & customer portals

Sites that load fast on a patchy 4G connection, and portals where your customers check their own orders, invoices and tickets instead of calling you.

Suited to Firms selling to businesses, clinics, colleges, distributors

ERP & CRM systems

Dynamics 365 and Power Platform implementations, custom plugins, data migration, and integrations that connect what you already run. Also full ERP built from scratch when the licensing maths doesn't work.

Suited to Manufacturers, healthcare groups, education, real estate, distribution
